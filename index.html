what we do

Cold email. Written by hand. Every single one.

Every other agency is racing to automate, AI-ify, and blast a million inboxes a day. We went the other direction. We pick prospects by hand, write every email from scratch, and manage every reply ourselves. It takes longer. It books more meetings. That is the whole point.

the organic promise

100% hand-picked leads.

A real human opens real databases and picks real prospects — one at a time. No bulk exports. No guesswork. Every name on the list is there for a reason.

100% hand-written copy.

No GPT templates. No "Hi {{first_name}}, I came across your {{company}}." Every email is written for one person, from scratch — the kind of message people actually reply to.

We verify every address before it goes out. Bounce rates stay near zero. Your domain stays healthy. Your sender reputation stays intact.

100% hand-managed replies.

A human reads every reply and handles it. No auto-responder. No bot. The person who wrote the email is the person who follows it up.

why this matters

Buyers are deleting your competitors' emails without reading them.

Their inboxes are drowning in AI-written sequences that all sound the same. They have developed a reflex: see a cold email, delete it. The ones that still get read — and replied to — are the ones that feel like they came from a person who actually did their homework.

That is all we do. We send fewer emails than anyone in the industry and book more meetings per send than anyone in the industry. Slow and deliberate beats fast and sloppy every time.

how the work actually gets done

lead sourcing A human opens Apollo, LinkedIn, Crunchbase, niche directories, podcast guest lists, conference attendee lists. Every prospect is picked individually. No "export 50,000 contacts" button — ever.
enrichment We read the company site. We read the prospect's recent posts and interviews. We find one specific, genuine reason to reach out — the kind of detail no AI scraper would notice.
email verification Every address gets checked before it goes out. Catch-all or risky? We double-check or drop it. Your domain reputation is not a number we gamble with.
copywriting Written by a person, for a person. No spintax. No mail-merge tokens standing in for research. A short, specific message that earns a reply because it deserves one.
sending Slow, warmed, monitored. Real schedules, real volumes — the kind a real human could plausibly send. Nothing that trips a spam filter or raises a red flag.
replies A human reads every reply. A human responds. A human books the call. The conversation never gets handed to a bot.
